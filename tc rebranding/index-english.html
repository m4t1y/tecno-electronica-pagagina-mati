help FAQ

FAQ

Answers to common questions about our services, installations, and technical workflow.

How do I request a quote? expand_more
You can complete the contact form on this page, text us via WhatsApp, or send an email. We reply with a detailed proposal within 48 business hours.
What is your service coverage area? expand_more
We are based in Tanti, Córdoba, Argentina. We perform on-site installations and technical services throughout the entire province of Córdoba.
Is the automatic school bell compatible with my existing bell? expand_more
Yes, our system is universal and integrates smoothly with most existing electric bells. We conduct an assessment to guarantee 100% compatibility.
What happens during a power outage? expand_more
The automatic bell system features non-volatile memory. All schedule configurations remain safe and automatically resume when power returns.
Can each service be customized? expand_more
Absolutely. Every project is tailored to the specific needs of the client. We perform a preliminary assessment to design the best solution.
Do you provide post-installation support? expand_more
Yes, we provide technical support after every setup. We include staff training and prompt assistance for any operational questions.
Is an ongoing internet connection required? expand_more
Not for all services. Once programmed, the automatic school bell operates completely autonomously. Network access is only required for remote schedule updates.
